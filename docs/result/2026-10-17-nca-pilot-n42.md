## Caveats
- n = 1 run. The interval is over validation images; the training-seed noise (about 0.003-0.005) is not in it. The sign is stable across the four epochs and both filters, which a seed fluke would not guarantee but makes unlikely.
- Hyper-parameters (tau, bank, queue, lambda, cap, gate) are the untuned defaults fixed before the run. This tests that one design, not every class-conditional memory loss.
- The internal validation split is not patient-disjoint (77% of val images share a patient with train) and is 30% lateral. The leaderboard set is 3,020 frontal images; the frontal rows are the closer proxy and tell the same story.
- Tail rows rest on 7 / 9 / 29 / 49 / 58 / 71 / 140 positives; only differences above about 0.02-0.03 are resolvable there.
- The job ran on node colossus (the launcher excludes it by default because of earlier GPU trouble); the log shows no CUDA error, NaN or traceback, and the run finished 0:0. The two validation images that cannot be decoded (`00006390`, `00006973`, "cv2.imdecode returned None") are logged every epoch, as in the other runs.

## Notable
- The first memory loss that is demonstrably live (the old triplet term was active on about 1% of hinges, 2-10% of the ASL gradient) does not help; it hurts. So "the term was inactive" explained the old null, but making it active at ASL-comparable magnitude on the per-class embeddings moves the model in a worse direction. This contradicts the working expectation in the plan that a dense class-conditional loss would add 0.3-1.5 mAP points.
- SWA over epochs 2-4 raises this run's validation loss to 0.0438 (single epochs 0.0392-0.0407) and its mECE to 0.1807; for ASL and Entropy SWA sits at or below the per-epoch values.
- The probability inflation (mean probability on negatives +32% over ASL) is the mechanism of the mECE rise.

## Not measured
- The `na` (no gate) and `nr` (random gate) controls: whether the gate matters, or the loss itself harms, is open.
- Other seeds and other (tau, lambda, queue) settings; the plan rules both out for the paper.
- The plan's untested guess that a rare class's 128-entry positive queue holds a handful of images seen many times and the soft-neighbour target memorises them (the loss falls while validation does not follow; no queue-composition diagnostic was run).
- A flip-TTA evaluation of `model_best.pth` (EMA, epoch 4), which would separate the SWA effect on calibration from the loss effect (GPU, about 36 min; not run).
